Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you visit or make a purchase from elysehartnett.com.

1. Information Collected

When you place an order or sign up for updates, the following information may be collected:

  • Name
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Email address
  • Payment information
  • Order details

Payments are processed securely through third-party providers, including Stripe and PayPal. Elyse Hartnett does not store or have direct access to full credit card or financial account information.

When you browse this website, certain non-identifiable information may also be collected automatically, such as browser type, device type, or general geographic region, for analytics and website performance purposes.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Personal information is used to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Provide shipping updates
  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Improve website functionality
  • Send occasional studio updates if you choose to subscribe

Marketing emails are sent only if you voluntarily opt in. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in any email.

3. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may be shared only with trusted third-party service providers necessary to operate this business, including:

  • Payment processors (Stripe and PayPal)
  • Shipping carriers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Email marketing services (if applicable)

These providers have access only to the information necessary to perform their services.

4. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect personal information. Payment transactions are encrypted and processed securely by Stripe and PayPal.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance user experience and collect basic analytics data. You may disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting:

elysehartnett@gmail.com

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

elysehartnett@gmail.com
